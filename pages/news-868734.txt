Project Overview 本项目为俄罗斯大型石油公司（Rosneft / 俄罗斯石油公司）旗下年产40万吨高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）装置配套的粉粒体密相气力输送系统。该项目对高压旋转阀的密封性能、耐压等级、计量精度及长期运行可靠性提出了极高要求，涉及从聚合反应出料、挤压造粒到成品均化掺混工段的全流程物料输送。 This project is a dense-phase pneumatic conveying system for the powder and pellet handling of a 400,000 t/y high-density polyethylene (HDPE) plant owned by Russia’s largest oil company (Rosneft). The project demanded exceptionally high sealing performance, pressure rating, metering accuracy, and long-term reliability from the high-pressure rotary valves, covering the entire material flow from polymerization discharge, extrusion pelletizing, to finished-product homogenization and blending. 项目原设计方案中，高压旋转阀指定采用德国进口ZVH系列旋转阀。然而，由于国际制裁及供应链限制，德国进口ZVH系列旋转阀的采购未能成功。在此情况下，上海科全工程技术有限公司凭借多年深耕气力输送领域的技术积淀与制造能力，其自主研发生产的GVH系列高压旋转阀通过了严格的性能审查与对比测试，成功获得业主及工程承包商认可，实现了对德国进口旋转阀的替代，并于2004年签订供货合同。 In the original design, the high-pressure rotary valves were specified as the German-imported ZVH series. GVH Series High-Pressure Rotary Valve Technical Solution 3.1 密相气力输送中旋转阀的核心作用 3.1 Core Role of the Rotary Valve in Dense-Phase Conveying 在密相气力输送系统中，高压旋转阀（High Pressure Rotary Valve）承担两项核心功能：一是定量供料，将料仓中的PE颗粒按工艺要求的流量均匀、连续地送入输送管道；二是锁气密封，在阀体上下游维持设定压力差，有效减少高压压缩空气向上泄漏，从而保证 motive air 有效用于物料输送而非损耗。 In a dense-phase pneumatic conveying system, the high-pressure rotary valve performs two core functions: (1) quantitative feeding—uniformly and continuously delivering PE pellets from the silo into the conveying line at the process-required flow rate; and (2) air-locking—maintaining the set pressure differential across the valve to minimize leakage of high-pressure compressed air upward, ensuring that motive air is effectively used for material transport rather than lost. 与稀相输送相比，密相输送采用更高的工作压力（通常>1.03 bar(g)）和更低的气量，物料在管道中以集团流（plug flow）或沙丘流（dune flow）状态推进，颗粒速度低、料气比高，对PE这类易破碎的颗粒料尤为适合。 Compared with dilute-phase conveying, dense-phase conveying employs higher operating pressure (typically >1.03 bar(g)) and lower air volume. The material advances through the pipeline in plug flow or dune flow regimes with low particle velocity and high solid-loading ratio, making it particularly suitable for friable pellet materials such as PE. 3.2 GVH系列高压旋转阀——替代进口的技术底气 3.2 GVH Series—The Technical Confidence to Replace Imports GVH系列是上海科全工程技术有限公司针对高压差、大产能密相输送工况自主研发生产的高压旋转阀产品。该系列对标德国德国进口ZVH系列同等规格，在结构设计、材料选型、密封系统及性能指标上经过严格对标与优化，成功通过Daelim及业主的严苛审查，成为该项目的正式供货方案。其关键技术特点如下： The GVH series is a high-pressure rotary valve independently developed and manufactured by Shanghai Conque for high-pressure-differential, large-capacity dense-phase conveying duties. The AMU precisely distributes supplemental air into the conveying line, ensuring that material is transported smoothly at low velocity and high concentration, preventing pipeline blockage and material degradation. 该系统配置使得输送管道弯头磨损显著降低，管道使用寿命延长；同时，PE颗粒的破碎率与粉化率大幅下降，成品质量稳定，符合高端聚烯烃产品对粉尘含量的严格要求。 This system configuration significantly reduces elbow wear in the conveying pipeline and extends pipeline service life.
