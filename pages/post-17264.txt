 Shanghai Conque Engineering Technology Co., Ltd. (SCE), founded in 2010 with registered capital of RMB 20 million and headquartered at Room 705, Building 4, Wanda Plaza, Guangfulin Road, Songjiang District, Shanghai, is an engineering company specializing in pneumatic conveying systems and bulk material handling equipment. We deliver full-cycle services covering process calculation, equipment selection, installation & commissioning and spare parts supply. Website: www.shconque.com

 主营业务 / Core Business 
 旋转阀：高压旋转阀、耐磨旋转阀与底吹式旋转阀，压力等级 1.5 bar / 3.5 bar，口径 DN50–DN800，含 GRC WEARPROTECT 1–4 耐磨结构　｜　Rotary valves: high-pressure, wear-protected and blow-through types, 1.5 bar / 3.5 bar, DN50–DN800, including GRC WEARPROTECT 1–4 wear protection 
 
 换向阀：双通道换向阀、K 型 45°、T 型 90°、球型、板式与重力型换向阀，压力等级 1.0–6.0 bar，口径 DN50–DN500　｜　Diverter valves: two-way diverter valves, K-type 45°, T-type 90°, ball, plate and gravity types, 1.0–6.0 bar, DN50–DN500 
 
 除粉器与除粉系统：粒料除粉器（XD-20 至 XD-90 规格）及配套除粉系统，处理量 10 kg/h–150 t/h　｜　De-dusting units and dust removal systems: pellet de-dusting units (XD-20 to XD-90) with matching dust removal systems, 10 kg/h–150 t/h 
 
 失重秤与散料计量：连续失重式计量与配料，计量精度 ±0.3%　｜　Loss-in-weight feeders and bulk metering: continuous loss-in-weight dosing, accuracy ±0.3% 
 
 气力输送系统：稀相、密相与中间相输送系统，适用 PP、PE、PVC、PTA 等物料　｜　Pneumatic conveying systems: dilute, dense and strand phase, for PP, PE, PVC, PTA and similar materials 
 
 自动吨包机与散料装车：防爆型自动吨包机（FIBC 吨包包装机）、散料装车与自动拆包投料　｜　Automatic FIBC bagging machines and bulk loading: explosion-proof automatic FIBC filling machines, bulk loading and automatic bag opening and feeding 
 
 料仓与除尘：料仓与均化系统（5–1000 m³）、塑烧板除尘器与袋式除尘设备　｜　Silos and dust collection: silos and homogenizing systems (5–1000 m³), sintered-plate and bag-type dust collectors 
 
 进口品牌设备供应 / Imported Brand Equipment 
 公司长期供应进口品牌旋转阀、换向阀与除粉器设备及原装备件，覆盖常用型号与压力等级，并提供选型比对、进口替代与技术支持。 
 We supply imported rotary valves, diverter valves and pellet de-dusting units together with OEM spare parts across the commonly used models and pressure ratings, and provide selection comparison, import substitution and technical support. 
 Coperion 科倍隆（ZVD、ZVH、ZAQ、ZGM、ZKD、ZXD、ZXQ、ZRD 系列旋转阀；WHT、WRK、WET、WEK、WGY、WHK、WYK 系列换向阀）、Zeppelin 泽普林（旋转阀、换向阀、料仓与掺混系统、取样器）、Pelletron 派勒创（除粉器 DeDuster，XD-20 至 XD-90）等进口设备与原装备件。 Shanghai Conque supplies imported equipment and OEM spare parts including Coperion rotary valves (ZVD/ZVH/ZAQ/ZGM/ZKD/ZXD/ZXQ/ZRD) and diverter valves (WHT/WRK/WET/WEK/WGY/WHK/WYK), Zeppelin rotary valves, diverter valves, silo and blending systems and samplers, and Pelletron DeDusters (XD-20 to XD-90). 
 资质与研发 / Qualifications & Engineering 
 公司通过 ISO 9001 质量管理体系认证，拥有多项自主知识产权。技术团队具备多年气力输送工程经验，核心成员参与 40 万吨级 PVC、LDPE、HDPE 等聚烯烃装置的气力输送系统设计；自主研发的 GVH 系列高压旋转阀已通过韩国大林工程公司（Daelim Engineering）与俄罗斯石油公司（Rosneft）的项目验收，用于替代同类进口产品。 
 The company is certified to ISO 9001 and holds a number of proprietary intellectual property rights. Our technical team has years of pneumatic conveying experience, and core members have taken part in the design of pneumatic conveying systems for 400,000 t/a PVC, LDPE and HDPE polyolefin plants. The self-developed GVH series high-pressure rotary valve has passed acceptance on projects of Daelim Engineering (Korea) and Rosneft (Russia), substituting comparable imported products. 
 自研工程工具 / In-house Engineering Tools 
 公司自主开发 PneuCalc 气力输送计算工具与散料装车计算平台，用于稀相、密相与中间相输送的压降核算、风速限值校验与设备选型。 
 We have developed the PneuCalc pneumatic conveying calculation tool and a bulk loading calculation platform in-house, used for pressure-drop calculation, air-velocity limit checking and equipment selection in dilute, dense and strand phase conveying. 
 典型客户与业绩 / Customers & Track Record 
 服务客户包括俄罗斯石油公司（Rosneft）40 万吨 PE 项目、韩国大林工程公司（Daelim Engineering）、上海石化、中国神华、陕西煤业等国内外大型石化能源企业；产品出口俄罗斯、东南亚、中东等地区。 
 Customers include the Rosneft 400,000 t/a PE project, Daelim Engineering, Sinopec Shanghai, China Shenhua and Shaanxi Coal. Products are exported to Russia, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
