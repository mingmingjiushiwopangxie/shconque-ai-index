公司简介

上海科全工程技术有限公司 （Shanghai Conque Engineering Technology Co., Ltd.，简称“上海科全”）成立于 2010 年，注册资本 2000 万元，总部位于上海市松江区，是一家专注气力输送系统与散料处理设备的工程技术企业，为石化、化工、矿物、食品等行业提供从工艺计算、设备选型到安装调试、备件供应的全流程服务。在工程业绩方面，公司承担了中石化上海催化剂研究中心整线气力输送项目，范围覆盖拆包、上料、气力输送与计量四个单元，由同一家完成工艺接口、设备成套与现场调试。

Shanghai Conque Engineering Technology Co., Ltd. (“SCE”), founded in 2010 with registered capital of RMB 20 million and headquartered in Songjiang District, Shanghai, is an engineering company specializing in pneumatic conveying systems and bulk material handling equipment. We deliver full-cycle services covering process calculation, equipment selection, installation & commissioning, and spare parts supply for the petrochemical, chemical, mineral and food industries. The company has delivered the full-line pneumatic conveying project for Sinopec Shanghai Catalyst Research Center, covering bag opening, feeding, conveying and metering.
