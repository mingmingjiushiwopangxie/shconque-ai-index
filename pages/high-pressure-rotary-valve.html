什么是高压旋转气锁阀 What Is a High-Pressure Rotary Airlock Valve

高压旋转阀（High-Pressure Rotary Valve），在工程上也叫 高压旋转气锁阀 、高压旋转供料器，是安装在料仓（或料斗）与气力输送管道之间、依靠旋转叶轮在承压管道与常压料仓之间建立动态压力隔离的容积式供料设备。它在系统中同时完成三件事：把物料定量喂入输送管道、阻止管道内压缩空气反窜入料仓、在阀前后持续压差下维持稳定锁气。 A high-pressure rotary valve (High-Pressure Rotary Valve), also called a high-pressure rotary airlock valve, or high-pressure rotary feeder, is a volumetric feeding device installed between a silo (or hopper) and a pneumatic conveying line. Its rotating rotor maintains a dynamic pressure seal between the pressurised line and the atmospheric silo, doing three jobs at once: metering material into the line, preventing compressed air from blowing back into the silo, and holding a stable air seal across a continuous differential.

当系统压差达到 1.5 bar 以上——密相输送常见 1.5～3.5 bar，高压密相与长距离输送可达 5～6 bar——普通直落式旋转阀的间隙漏气量、结构刚度和轴端密封都不足以支撑锁气要求，必须改用高压系列：叶片间隙收窄至 0.05～0.10 mm 并可调，侧盖内布置多重组合密封，轴承完全外置与物料隔离，同时强制配置排气口（Vent Port）。 Once the system differential reaches 1.5 bar or more — 1.5–3.5 bar is common in dense phase, and 5–6 bar for high-pressure dense phase and long-distance systems — an ordinary drop-through rotary valve can no longer hold the seal: tip leakage, structural stiffness and shaft-end sealing are all inadequate. A high-pressure series valve is required, with tip clearance tightened to an adjustable 0.05–0.10 mm, several sealing stages inside the end cover, bearings fully outboard and isolated from the product, and a mandatory vent port.

高压旋转阀结构与锁气原理 Construction and Airlock Principle

高压锁气不是靠“堵”，而是靠“气”——叶轮几何 + 多重轴端密封 + 轴气封 + 排气口构成的组合结构。 High-pressure sealing is not achieved by blocking but by air — a combination of rotor geometry, multiple shaft-end seals, air purge and a vent port.

叶轮壳体密封结构 Rotor, Housing and Seal Arrangement

高压旋转阀由壳体、转子（叶轮）、侧盖、传动与轴密封系统四部分构成。与低压阀的核心结构差异，在转子轴两端的密封布置：低压阀用填料加迷宫即可，高压阀必须在侧盖内布置一个独立的高压密封腔，并沿轴向串接多道密封。 A high-pressure rotary valve comprises four parts: housing, rotor (impeller), end covers, and drive and shaft sealing. The key difference from a low-pressure valve lies at the two rotor shaft ends: a low-pressure valve needs only packing plus a labyrinth, whereas a high-pressure valve must have a dedicated high-pressure seal chamber inside the end cover, with several sealing stages in series along the shaft.

壳体： FEM 优化铸件壳体（常用 QT500-7 球墨铸铁），在减重的同时提升刚度，保证压差下转子与壳体间隙稳定不漂移 FEM-optimised cast housing (typically QT500-7 nodular iron): lighter yet stiffer, keeping rotor-to-housing clearance stable under differential pressure

转子（叶轮）： 8 / 10 / 14 片，高压密相常用 14 片开放式设计，外缘无接触式密封件，避免高压下密封件快速磨损；可选防卡料鹰嘴装置处理异形颗粒或含玻纤物料 Rotor (impeller): 8 / 10 / 14 pockets; 14-pocket open design is common for high-pressure dense phase, with no contacting seal at the rim to avoid rapid seal wear; an anti-jamming beak is optional for irregular granules or glass-fibre-bearing materials

侧盖高压密封腔： 沿轴向依次为机械密封（或盘根/填料密封）+ 油封 + 迷宫密封 + 轴气封（Air Purge），轴承完全外置并与物料隔离 End-cover high-pressure seal chamber: along the shaft: mechanical seal (or gland/packing) + oil seal + labyrinth seal + air purge, with bearings fully outboard and isolated from the product

叶片间隙： 低压工况 0.10～0.30 mm；高压工况收窄至 0.05～0.10 mm，并推荐采用可调间隙结构，保留现场微调余量 Tip clearance: 0.10–0.30 mm at low pressure; tightened to 0.05–0.10 mm at high pressure, preferably with an adjustable-clearance design for on-site fine tuning

传动： 链条传动（大扭矩、需调速）或直连型（结构紧凑、维护简便），配套变频器实现无级调速与扭矩监测 chain drive (high torque, speed control needed) or direct-coupled (compact, easy to maintain), with a VFD for stepless speed control and torque monitoring

高压差工况气封吹扫设计 Air Purge and Vent Design for High Differential

高压差下细粉会沿转轴向外渗透，靠单一的接触式密封必然快速磨损。工程上的做法是 把“堵”改成“挡 + 排” ：用一股持续的洁净气在密封副前面形成气幕挡粉，再用排气口把携气排走，密封件只承担残余压差，寿命因此显著延长。这套设计由三个部件构成。 At high differential, fine powder migrates outward along the shaft, and a single contact seal will inevitably wear quickly. Standard practice is to replace "blocking" with "barrier + purge": a continuous flow of clean air forms an air curtain in front of the sealing faces to keep powder out, while a vent port carries the carried-over gas away. The seal then only sees the residual differential, which greatly extends its life. Three components make up this design.

轴气封（Air Purge）： 持续向密封腔供入洁净干燥的仪表风，压力略高于腔内压力（工程上常按高出 0.2～0.5 bar 设定），在轴套表面形成气幕，阻挡细粉进入机械密封或盘根的密封副 Air purge (Air Purge): clean, dry instrument air is fed continuously into the seal chamber at a pressure slightly above chamber pressure (typically 0.2–0.5 bar higher), forming an air curtain on the shaft sleeve that keeps fine powder out of the mechanical seal or gland

排气口（Vent Port，俗称吹扫孔）： 高压阀必须配置。转子格腔在承压侧会携走一部分输送气，没有排气口释放，这部分气体在格腔内形成气阻——填充率下降、输送量不足，气体还会沿阀体窜入料仓，导致料仓顶压力异常、除尘器超载。排气应释放回料仓或独立过滤器/消音器 Vent port (also called a purge port): mandatory on high-pressure valves. Pockets on the pressurised side carry conveying gas; without a vent, that gas builds an air lock in the pocket — filling efficiency and throughput drop, and gas also migrates through the valve body into the silo, causing abnormal silo-top pressure and dust-collector overload. Vent back to the silo or to a dedicated filter/silencer

吹扫气接口： 针对粉煤灰、水泥等易粘附物料，可配弹性叶片或吹扫气接口，在不拆阀的情况下在线清理转子与格腔 Purge air connection: for sticky materials such as fly ash and cement, flexible tips or a purge-air connection allow the rotor and pockets to be cleaned online without dismantling the valve

易被忽略的失效链： 轴气封的气源若带水、带油，或在梅雨季未做干燥处理，密封腔内的粉料会因微量含湿而粘连板结。积料逐步侵占密封腔容积后，先是轴封微漏、轴承室进粉、运行扭矩缓慢上升，继续运行则盘根与机械密封动静环被硬颗粒磨穿，泄漏量骤增，极端情况下可能抱轴卡死。 因此轴气封属于每日点检项 ：确认三联件（过滤-减压-油雾）工作正常、气源洁净干燥、轴封处无微漏、扭矩无缓慢上升趋势。

An easily overlooked failure chain: if the purge air carries water or oil, or is not dried during the rainy season, powder in the seal chamber sticks and cakes. As the deposit eats into the chamber volume, the first symptoms are slight shaft-seal leakage, powder entering the bearing housing and a slow rise in running torque; continued operation then wears through the gland and the mechanical seal faces, leakage rises sharply, and in extreme cases the shaft seizes.Air purge is therefore a daily check item: confirm the FRL unit (filter-regulator-lubricator) is working, the air supply is clean and dry, there is no slight leakage at the shaft seal, and torque shows no slow upward trend.

正压粉体输送锁气不漏风 Leak-Tight Airlock for Positive-Pressure Powder Conveying

旋转阀在正压系统里承担的是气锁（airlock）功能：必须把承压管道与常压料仓隔开，同时把物料连续送入管道。这意味着两件事同时发生——物料通过格腔向下供料，高压气体则沿转子与壳体的间隙、以及格腔回流通道向上泄漏。第二条被忽略的结果，就是风机的选型风量被低估。 In a positive-pressure system the rotary valve acts as an airlock: it must separate the pressurised line from the atmospheric silo while feeding material continuously into the line. Two things happen at once — material is fed down through the pockets, while high-pressure gas leaks upward through the rotor-to-housing clearance and the pocket return passage. Ignoring the second effect is how fan capacity gets undersized.

Q_leak ≈ C_d × A_gap × √(2 × ΔP / ρ_gas)

C_d 为流量系数（经验取 0.6～0.8），A_gap 为有效泄漏间隙面积，ΔP 为阀前后压差，ρ_gas 为气体密度。从这个简化式可以读出三条对选型有直接影响的结论： C_d is the discharge coefficient (0.6–0.8 in practice), A_gap the effective leakage clearance area, ΔP the differential across the valve and ρ_gas the gas density. Three conclusions that directly affect selection follow from this simplified expression:

漏气量与 √ΔP 成正比： 压差从 1.5 bar 升到 3.5 bar，漏气量约增加 53% ，而不是线性增长 Leakage is proportional to √ΔP: raising the differential from 1.5 bar to 3.5 bar increases leakage by about 53%, not linearly

漏气量与间隙面积成正比： 叶片间隙从 0.10 mm 放大到 0.30 mm，漏气量增至约 3 倍 Leakage is proportional to clearance area: opening tip clearance from 0.10 mm to 0.30 mm increases leakage about threefold

漏气量与气体密度相关： 氮气闭路输送需按质量流量单独核算，并同步校核补氮量 Leakage depends on gas density: closed-loop nitrogen conveying must be calculated separately on a mass-flow basis, with make-up nitrogen verified at the same time

If the design simply carries over a single-valve estimate of 10%, start-up typically brings "throughput at only 70% of design and high silo-top pressure". 耐磨材质适应磨蚀性粉料 Wear-Resistant Materials for Abrasive Powders

催化剂、氧化铝、PTA 粉料、碳酸钙与滑石粉等物料对转子外缘和壳体流道的磨蚀是持续性的，磨损直接表现为间隙扩大、漏气量上升、输送量衰减。耐磨方案不止是“换个硬材料”，而是一组组合措施。 Catalyst, alumina, PTA powder, calcium carbonate and talc abrade the rotor rim and housing passages continuously; wear shows up as growing clearance, rising leakage and falling throughput. A wear-resistant solution is not simply "a harder material" but a set of combined measures.

转子硬化： 表面硬化处理或碳化钨堆焊（Hard-facing），重点作用于叶片外缘与端面 Rotor hardening: surface hardening or tungsten-carbide hard-facing, focused on the tip edges and end faces

粉体处理量、口径选型参考 Throughput and Size Selection

口径不能只看“输送能力 200 t/h”这类样本上限值，而应由目标输送量反算所需理论体积流量后再确定。旋转阀的容积能力按下式计算： Do not size from catalogue maxima such as "capacity 200 t/h"; work back from the target throughput to the required theoretical volumetric flow. Rotary valve volumetric capacity is:

V_th = 60 × V_rotor × n × η_v　（m³/h）

V_rotor 为转子单格容积（m³/r），n 为转速（r/min，常规 20～60），η_v 为容积效率。理论质量输送量再乘以填充率与物料堆积密度： V_rotor is the rotor pocket volume (m³/r), n the speed (r/min, typically 20–60) and η_v the volumetric efficiency. Theoretical mass throughput then follows from filling factor and bulk density:

Q = V_th × φ × ρ_b　（t/h）

转速： 常规 20～60 r/min，建议变频调速；转速过高会加剧磨损与颗粒破碎 Typically 20–60 r/min, VFD recommended; excessive speed accelerates wear and particle breakage

填充率 φ： 一般控制在 30%～60%。密相取 30%～50%（防剪切破碎），稀相可取 40%～60%；低于 30% 说明阀选大了，高于 60% 说明阀选小了 Generally 30%–60%. Use 30%–50% for dense phase (to avoid shear breakage) and 40%–60% for dilute phase; below 30% means the valve is oversized, above 60% means it is undersized

容积效率 η_v： 低压差（≤1.5 bar）可取 0.85～0.95；高压差（≥3.5 bar）密相建议取 0.60～0.75。把容积效率与填充率分开取值，是避免产能误判的关键 Volumetric efficiency η_v: 0.85–0.95 at low differential (≤1.5 bar); 0.60–0.75 for dense phase at high differential (≥3.5 bar). Setting volumetric efficiency and filling factor separately is the key to avoiding capacity misjudgement

裕量系数 K： 取 1.1～1.3，已含漏气导致的充填下降，V_design = V_req × K Margin factor K: 1.1–1.3, already allowing for the filling loss caused by leakage; V_design = V_req × K

算例： 某型号样本给出理论体积流量 V_th = 40 m³/h，物料为 PE 颗粒（堆积密度约 560 kg/m³），填充率取 45%，则 Q ≈ 40 × 0.45 × 0.56 ≈ 10.1 t/h 。反算时须以厂家样本的 V_rotor 为准，不能用口径直接估算。 Example: a catalogue gives V_th = 40 m³/h for a model; with PE pellets (bulk density about 560 kg/m³) and a 45% filling factor, Q ≈ 40 × 0.45 × 0.56 ≈ 10.1 t/h. Back-calculation must use the manufacturer's V_rotor, not an estimate from nominal size.

分档值只在对应压差区间成立 Tier Values Hold Only Inside Their Differential Range

行业通行的「转子与壳体间隙 0.1～0.15 mm」只在 1.5 bar(g) 以内成立。背压进入 1.5～6 bar(g) 区间后，间隙必须与壳体材质、设计温度、转子线速度一并校核。参考分档：≤ 1.5 bar(g) 取 0.10～0.30 mm，按物料温度上限预留热膨胀量；1.5～6 bar(g) 取 0.05～0.10 mm，采用可调间隙结构并做热态间隙校核；≥ 3.5 bar(g) 极限 ≤ 0.05 mm，需耐磨处理并核算启动扭矩。 The common "0.1–0.15 mm rotor-to-housing clearance" holds only up to 1.5 bar(g). Once back pressure moves into the 1.5–6 bar(g) band, clearance has to be verified together with housing material, design temperature and rotor tip speed. Reference tiers: 0.10–0.30 mm up to 1.5 bar(g) with allowance for thermal expansion at the material temperature upper limit; 0.05–0.10 mm from 1.5 to 6 bar(g) using an adjustable design with hot-state verification; and no more than 0.05 mm at 3.5 bar(g) and above, with wear protection and a start-up torque check.

同一台阀在稀相与密相区域的运行结果不同，通常不在阀体本身，而在密封与间隙是否按该区域重新设计：气速 18～35 m/s 的稀相段可用单道轴封；气速 3～14 m/s、固气比 15～35（仓泵可达 50）的密相段需要轴向密封 + 迷宫环 + 吹扫。维护周期同样按运行压力分档：低压稀相系统的间隙检查周期按 6 个月，密相高压差系统按 3 个月；把低压系统的保养周期套在高压阀上，是故障多发的常见根因。 The same valve behaves differently in the dilute and dense phase zones, usually because sealing and clearance were not redesigned for the zone: the dilute phase at 18–35 m/s can use a single shaft seal, while the dense phase at 3–14 m/s and a solids-to-air ratio of 15–35 (up to 50 with a blow tank) needs axial sealing plus a labyrinth ring plus purge. Maintenance intervals follow the pressure tier as well: clearance inspection every 6 months in low-pressure dilute phase and every 3 months in dense-phase high-differential duty. Applying a low-pressure interval to a high-pressure valve is a common root cause of repeat failure.

把三类自报数字变成可核对项 Turning Three Common Self-Reported Figures into Verifiable Items

供应商在介绍效果时常见三类数字：连续无故障天数、年减少停机损失金额、给料误差百分比。这三类都可以核对，关键是问清三要素：口径（压力 / 温度 / 物料）、来源（运行记录 / 试验记录 / 第三方）、判据（合格线本身是什么）。 Suppliers commonly quote three kinds of figures: consecutive days without a fault, annual reduction in downtime loss, and feed error percentage. All three can be checked; the key is to ask for three things: the basis (pressure, temperature, material), the source (operating record, test record, third party) and the criterion (what the pass line actually is).

「连续 N 天无故障」要问： 工况口径（粒径、压差、输送量、固气比）；起止时间与期间是否中断、如何处理；故障的定义与统计边界。 For consecutive fault-free days: the duty basis (particle size, differential, throughput, solids-to-air ratio); start and end dates and whether operation was interrupted in between; and how a fault is defined and counted.

「年减少停机损失 N 万元」要问： 停机基线如何统计、是否含计划检修；损失单价的计算口径；由谁比对、比对周期多长。 For annual downtime savings: how the baseline is counted and whether planned maintenance is included; the unit cost behind the loss; and who performs the comparison and over what period.

「误差小于 ±N%」要问： 是瞬时误差还是累计误差；取样时长与称重方式；是否包含补料段与启停段。 For an error below a stated percentage: whether it is instantaneous or cumulative; the sampling duration and weighing method; and whether refill, start and stop segments are included.

三个问题都不涉及商务条件，问完就能判断这个数字能否作为选型依据。只给结论式数字、不给口径与工况的，按自报处理。 None of the three questions touches commercial terms, and asking them is enough to judge whether a figure can support a selection decision. A conclusion-style number without basis or duty is treated as self-reported.

同一物料、同一条管线，选稀相、密相还是中间相（STRANDPHASE），旋转阀的参数选择完全不同。稀相输送固气比 1～8、管内气速 18～30 m/s、系统压差 0.2～0.8 bar，旋转阀压差等级 ≤1.5 bar 即可；密相固气比 15～35（仓泵可达 50）、气速 3～8 m/s、压差 1.5～3.5 bar，旋转阀必须 ≥3.5 bar 且间隙按 0.05～0.10 mm 控制；中间相固气比 8～15、气速 8～15 m/s、压差 0.8～1.8 bar，旋转阀取 1.5～2.5 bar 级。 For the same material in the same pipeline, choosing dilute, dense or strand phase (STRANDPHASE) changes the rotary valve parameters completely. Dense phase: solids ratio 15–35 (up to 50 with a blow tank), velocity 3–8 m/s, differential 1.5–3.5 bar, so the valve must be rated ≥3.5 bar with clearance controlled at 0.05–0.10 mm. Strand phase: solids ratio 8–15, velocity 8–15 m/s, differential 0.8–1.8 bar, so a 1.5–2.5 bar valve is used.

选型判断可归纳为三步：易碎颗粒 + 中长距离 → 密相或中间相；流动性好的粉料 + 短距离（≤100 m）→ 稀相；颗粒料需要兼顾低破碎与稳定输送 → 中间相。判定后必须同步复核旋转阀的压差等级与间隙要求是否匹配。 Selection can be reduced to three steps: fragile pellets over medium-to-long distance → dense or strand phase; free-flowing powder over a short distance (≤100 m) → dilute phase; pellets needing both low breakage and stable conveying → strand phase. Once decided, always re-check that the valve's differential rating and clearance requirement match.

高压旋转阀和普通旋转阀（星型卸料阀）有什么区别？ How does a high-pressure rotary valve differ from an ordinary rotary (star discharge) valve?

核心差别在压差等级与轴端密封。普通旋转阀多用于 ≤1.5 bar 的常压卸料或稀相给料，叶片间隙 0.10～0.30 mm，填料加迷宫密封即可；高压旋转阀要承受 1.5～6 bar 压差，叶片间隙收窄到 0.05～0.10 mm 并常采用可调间隙结构，侧盖内布置机械密封（或盘根）+ 油封 + 迷宫 + 轴气封的多重组合密封，轴承完全外置与物料隔离，同时必须配置排气口（Vent Port）。 The difference lies in differential rating and shaft-end sealing. An ordinary rotary valve is used mainly for atmospheric discharge or dilute-phase feeding up to 1.5 bar, with 0.10–0.30 mm tip clearance and packing plus labyrinth sealing. A high-pressure valve withstands 1.5–6 bar, with clearance tightened to 0.05–0.10 mm and usually adjustable, multiple seals in the end cover (mechanical seal or gland, oil seal, labyrinth, air purge), bearings fully outboard and isolated from the product, and a mandatory vent port.

高压旋转阀的额定压差有哪几档？3.5 bar 系统应该选多大额定压差？ What differential ratings are available, and which rating should a 3.5 bar system use?

额定压差按 1.5～6 bar 分级覆盖，常见 1.5 bar / 3.5 bar / 5 bar / 6 bar 四档。选型红线是系统工作压差须控制在阀额定压差的 80% 以内；按此推算，3.5 bar 系统宜选 4.0～4.5 bar 额定级阀型。若选用 3.5 bar 额定阀，必须核算高压差下的启动扭矩（可达常压工况的 3～5 倍），并配置泄压旁通与变频软启动，叶片间隙控制在 0.05～0.10 mm。 Ratings are graded from 1.5 to 6 bar, with 1.5, 3.5, 5 and 6 bar the four common steps. The engineering rule is that system working differential must stay within 80% of the valve's rated differential; on that basis a 3.5 bar system should use a 4.0–4.5 bar rated valve. If a 3.5 bar rated valve is used, starting torque at high differential (up to 3–5 times the atmospheric-duty value) must be verified, with pressure-relief bypass and VFD soft start, and tip clearance held at 0.05–0.10 mm.

高压旋转阀的输送能力能到多少？应该怎么估算？ What is the maximum conveying capacity, and how should it be estimated?

单机输送能力上限约 200 t/h（与口径、转速、物料堆积密度强相关）；3.5 bar 标准型约 50～100 t/h。估算时不能只看样本上限值，应由目标输送量反算所需理论体积流量，再结合容积效率与填充率确定口径与转速：理论体积流量 = 格腔容积 × 格数 × 转速，容积效率低压差（≤1.5 bar）可取 0.85～0.95。反算须以厂家样本的转子容积为准，不能用口径直接估算。 Capacity reaches about 200 t/h per unit, strongly dependent on bore, rotor speed and bulk density; the 3.5 bar standard type gives about 50–100 t/h. Do not select from the catalogue maximum: back-calculate the required theoretical volumetric flow from the target throughput, then combine volumetric efficiency and filling factor to fix bore and speed — theoretical volume flow = pocket volume × number of pockets × rotor speed, with volumetric efficiency of 0.85–0.95 at low differential (≤1.5 bar). Always use the manufacturer's rotor volume for back-calculation, never the bore alone.

高压旋转阀为什么必须配排气口（Vent Port）？ Why is a vent port mandatory on a high-pressure rotary valve?

转子格腔在承压侧会携带一部分输送气体。没有排气口释放，这部分气体就在格腔内形成气阻：填充率下降、输送量不足；气体还会沿阀体进入料仓，导致料仓顶压力异常、除尘器超载，在多台阀向一条主管供料时尤其明显。排气口应把携气释放回料仓或独立过滤器/消音器。 Pockets on the pressurised side carry conveying gas. Without a vent to release it, that gas creates an air lock in the pocket: filling efficiency and throughput fall, and gas migrates through the valve into the silo, causing abnormal silo-top pressure and dust-collector overload — most pronounced when several valves feed one main line. The vent should release the carried-over gas back to the silo or to a dedicated filter or silencer.

高压旋转阀的漏气量怎么估算？ How is the leakage of a high-pressure rotary valve estimated?

工程上用 Q_leak ≈ C_d × A_gap × √(2ΔP/ρ) 做量级估算，C_d 取 0.6～0.8，A_gap 为有效泄漏间隙面积。三条结论：漏气量与 √ΔP 成正比（压差从 1.5 bar 升到 3.5 bar，漏气量约增加 53%）；与间隙面积成正比（间隙 0.10 mm 放大到 0.30 mm，漏气量增至约 3 倍）；与气体密度相关，氮气闭路输送需按质量流量单独核算。多点供料系统中漏气量可占风机总风量 30%～45%，必须逐台累加校核。 In practice Q_leak ≈ C_d × A_gap × √(2ΔP/ρ) gives an order-of-magnitude estimate, with C_d of 0.6–0.8 and A_gap the effective leakage clearance area. Three conclusions follow: leakage is proportional to √ΔP (raising differential from 1.5 bar to 3.5 bar increases leakage by about 53%); proportional to clearance area (0.10 mm to 0.30 mm roughly triples it); and gas-density dependent, so nitrogen closed-loop conveying must be checked separately on a mass-flow basis. In multi-point feeding, leakage can be 30%–45% of total fan flow and must be summed valve by valve.

高压旋转阀的叶片间隙取多少合适？ What tip clearance should a high-pressure rotary valve use?

全系列叶片间隙在 0.05～0.30 mm 之间；高压工况取 0.05～0.10 mm，3.5 bar 以上极限工况取 ≤0.05 mm 并需耐磨处理。高压系统推荐可调间隙结构：既保证泄漏可控，又保留现场微调余量。间隙过小会因热膨胀与物料夹持导致卡死，并非越小越好。 Tip clearance ranges from 0.05 to 0.30 mm across the family; high-pressure duties use 0.05–0.10 mm, and extreme duties at 3.5 bar and above use ≤0.05 mm with wear protection. Adjustable clearance is recommended for high-pressure service: leakage stays controllable while on-site fine tuning remains possible. Too small a clearance can seize through thermal expansion and trapped material — smaller is not automatically better.

高压旋转阀的转子转速和调速范围是多少？ What rotor speed and speed range does the valve use?

常规转子转速 20～60 r/min，配变频调速。密相工况建议取 20～40 r/min 低速运行，兼顾产能与寿命；转速是调节输送量的主要手段，与格腔容积共同决定体积流量。 Rotor speed is typically 20–60 r/min with VFD control. Dense-phase duties are best run slowly at 20–40 r/min to balance capacity against service life. Speed is the main means of adjusting throughput and, together with pocket volume, sets the volumetric flow.

高压旋转阀的轴端密封是什么结构？ What shaft-end sealing arrangement is used?

高压型采用多重组合密封：可调间隙密封 + 双唇轴封 + 轴气封，轴承完全外置与物料隔离；3.5 bar 以上极限工况可加端面机械密封（或盘根 + 油封 + 迷宫 + 轴气封组合）。轴气封需三联件工作正常、气源洁净干燥，属每日点检项；良好的轴封能把间隙泄漏控制成可计算、可重复的数值。 High-pressure units use a multiple seal arrangement: adjustable-clearance seal, double-lip shaft seal and air purge, with bearings fully outboard and isolated from the product; extreme duties above 3.5 bar may add a face mechanical seal (or a combination of gland, oil seal, labyrinth and air purge). The air purge needs a healthy FRL unit and clean, dry air and is a daily inspection item; good shaft sealing makes clearance leakage calculable and repeatable.

高温或粘性物料能用高压旋转阀吗？工作温域是多少？ Can high-pressure rotary valves handle hot or sticky materials, and over what temperature range?

工作温域为 −25 ℃～+250 ℃，高温或粘附性物料可选夹套伴热型，并对热膨胀与间隙变化单独校核；高压系统推荐可调间隙结构以保留现场微调余量。矿物与建材粉料等大产能工况用大口径配低速运行，并可设吹扫气接口在线清理粘附物料。 The working temperature range is −25 °C to +250 °C. For hot or sticky materials a jacketed design is available, with thermal expansion and clearance change checked separately; adjustable clearance is recommended for high-pressure service so on-site fine tuning remains possible. High-capacity mineral and building-material duties use large bores at low speed, optionally with a purge-air connection to clear adhering material online.

高压旋转阀有哪些材质和耐磨方案？ What materials and wear-protection options are available?

基体可选碳钢、不锈钢、316L 或双相钢；耐磨方案包括转子表面硬化、碳化钨堆焊与壳体镶陶瓷衬板，配合低速运行延长寿命。可按物料磨蚀性与介质要求组合，也可做夹套伴热、氮气轴气封与抛光定制。 Bodies are available in carbon steel, stainless steel, 316L or duplex; wear protection includes surface-hardened rotors, tungsten-carbide hardfacing and ceramic-lined housings, combined with low-speed operation to extend life. Options can be combined to suit material abrasiveness and media, along with jacketing, nitrogen air purge and polished finishes.

防爆场合的高压旋转阀怎么定制？ How is an explosion-proof high-pressure rotary valve specified?

采用 Ex d IIB/IIC T4 或 ATEX 认证配置，转子与壳体等电位接地防静电，配套氮气气封与氧含量监控实现惰化，并同步配置料仓顶泄爆片、管道阻火器、隔爆阀与扭矩监测过载保护。防爆方案需结合物料点火能下限（MIE）、粉尘云爆炸特性与现场区域划分确定。 Explosion-proof configurations use Ex d IIB/IIC T4 or ATEX-certified execution, equipotential rotor and housing bonding for static control, nitrogen purge with oxygen-content monitoring for inerting, plus silo-top explosion vents, pipeline flame arresters, explosion isolation valves and torque-monitored overload protection. The final scheme depends on the material's minimum ignition energy, dust-cloud explosion characteristics and the plant's hazardous-area classification.

高压旋转阀的法兰标准是什么？可以对接 DIN 或 ANSI 吗？ What flange standard is used, and can it match DIN or ANSI?

3.5 bar 高压旋转阀的标准法兰按 DIN PN10 或 ANSI 150 lbs 钻孔，可按项目要求选配；大口径与特殊压力等级也可按图定制。选型时请提供管道口径、压力等级与法兰标准，避免现场无法对接。 The 3.5 bar high-pressure rotary valve is drilled to DIN PN10 or ANSI 150 lbs as standard, selectable to project requirements, and large bores or special pressure ratings can be customised to drawings. 高压旋转阀适用哪些行业和物料？ Which industries and materials are high-pressure rotary valves used for?

从石化聚烯烃、新能源锂电材料、精细化工到矿物与建材粉料（碳酸钙、滑石粉、水泥、粉煤灰等）以及食品与塑料改性，凡是正压气力输送且需要锁气隔离的粉粒体工况都适用。腐蚀性、磨蚀性或易碎物料可分别通过材质、耐磨与进口几何设计适配。 Applications span petrochemical polyolefins, new-energy lithium-battery materials, fine chemicals, minerals and building materials (calcium carbonate, talc, cement, fly ash), food and plastics compounding — any powder or granular duty with positive-pressure pneumatic conveying that needs airlock isolation. Corrosive, abrasive or fragile materials are handled through material selection, wear protection and inlet-geometry design.

可以。氮气闭路输送通常配置氮气轴气封与氧含量监控实现惰化，漏气量须按气体密度与质量流量单独核算，并计入补气与回收系统。密闭输送时建议同时考虑夹套伴热与可调间隙结构，以适应温域变化。 Yes. Nitrogen closed-loop conveying normally uses a nitrogen air purge with oxygen-content monitoring for inerting; leakage must be calculated separately on a gas-density and mass-flow basis and included in the make-up and recovery system. The vent should release the carried-over gas back to the silo or to a dedicated filter or silencer.

高压旋转阀的漏气量怎么估算？ How is leakage estimated?

工程上用 Q_leak ≈ C_d × A_gap × √(2ΔP/ρ) 做量级估算，C_d 取 0.6～0.8，A_gap 为有效泄漏间隙面积。可读出三条结论：漏气量与 √ΔP 成正比（压差 1.5 bar 升到 3.5 bar，漏气量约增加 53%）；与间隙面积成正比（间隙 0.10 mm 放大到 0.30 mm，漏气量增至约 3 倍）；与气体密度相关，氮气闭路输送需按质量流量单独核算。多点供料系统中漏气量可占风机总风量 30%～45%，必须逐台累加校核。 In practice, Q_leak ≈ C_d × A_gap × √(2ΔP/ρ) gives an order-of-magnitude estimate, with C_d between 0.6 and 0.8 and A_gap the effective leakage clearance area. Three conclusions follow: leakage is proportional to √ΔP (raising the differential from 1.5 bar to 3.5 bar increases it by about 53%); proportional to clearance area (opening clearance from 0.10 mm to 0.30 mm increases it about threefold); and dependent on gas density, so closed-loop nitrogen systems must be calculated separately on a mass-flow basis. In multi-point feed systems leakage can take 30%–45% of fan flow and must be summed valve by valve.

换向阀在气力输送系统里起什么作用？ What does a diverter valve do in a pneumatic conveying system?

换向阀安装在输送管路的分支点，把物料从一条管线切换到另一条管线，用来实现多点分流与多点卸料——例如把成品分配到不同料仓、把不合格料切换到旁路，或在多条管线之间切换输送路径。双通道、Y 型、T 型、板式（筒式）、重力式与球型，都是换向阀的不同结构形式。 A diverter valve sits at a branch point of the conveying line and switches material from one pipeline to another, providing multi-point diverting and multi-point discharge — for example distributing finished product to different silos, routing off-spec material to a bypass, or switching the conveying path between lines. Twin-channel, Y type, T type, plate (cylinder), gravity and ball designs are all forms of diverter valve.

换向阀有哪些结构形式？分别适合什么场合？ Which diverter valve designs are available, and where is each used?

主要有双通道、Y 型、T 型、板式（筒式）、重力式与球型六类。双通道采用两条并列通道，结构紧凑、占地小，适用于粉料与颗粒物料；Y 型斜向分流、流动过渡平缓，阻力小、积料少，对物料形态保护好；T 型垂直分流、结构紧凑、切换行程短，适合空间受限的改造项目；板式（筒式）流通截面大，适合常规粉料与颗粒料分路；重力式依靠重力落料，适合大颗粒与大流量卸料；球型为球面密封、泄漏量低。 Six designs are available: twin-channel, Y type, T type, plate (cylinder), gravity and ball. Twin-channel uses two parallel passages — compact and space-saving for powders and pellets. Y type diverts at an angle, giving a gentle transition, low pressure loss, little accumulation and good protection of product form. T type diverts vertically — compact with a short switching stroke, suited to revamp projects with limited space. Plate (cylinder) type has a large flow area, for routine powder and pellet diverting. Gravity type relies on gravity discharge, for large particles and high-flow duty. Ball type uses a spherical seal with low leakage.

双通道换向阀的口径范围和工作压力是多少？ What are the size range and working pressure of the twin-channel diverter valve?

双通道换向阀公称口径覆盖 ND65 / ND80 / ND100 / ND125 / ND150 / ND175 / ND200 / ND250 共八档，工作压力 ≤ 5 barg，适用粉料与颗粒物料。阀芯形式可选圆柱型转子、摆动锤型、活塞式转轴、多通道或 V 型，并按磨损性与密封要求选择；可选右侧信号箱输出阀位反馈。 The twin-channel diverter valve covers eight nominal sizes — ND65 / ND80 / ND100 / ND125 / ND150 / ND175 / ND200 / ND250 — with a working pressure of ≤ 5 barg, for powders and pellets. Plug options include cylindrical rotor, swinging-hammer, piston shaft, multi-channel and V type, chosen to abrasiveness and sealing requirement; an optional right-hand signal box provides valve position feedback.

Y 型和 T 型换向阀有什么区别？ What is the difference between Y-type and T-type diverter valves?

Y 型采用斜向分流，物料沿斜向通道过渡，压损低、不易积料，对物料完整性保护好，适合 PP、PE 等易碎颗粒料输送；T 型采用垂直分流，结构紧凑、动作可靠、切换行程短，适合安装空间紧张、需要短行程切换的场合。Y 型口径覆盖 ND100～ND400，T 型覆盖 ND150～ND350；两者均适用于粉料与颗粒料，可与气力输送或重力卸料管路配套。 The Y type diverts at an angle, so material passes through a sloped passage with low pressure loss and little accumulation — gentle on product integrity and suited to friable pellets such as PP and PE. The T type diverts vertically — compact, reliable in action and short in switching stroke, suited to installations with limited space. The Y type covers ND100–ND400 and the T type ND150–ND350; both handle powders and pellets and suit pneumatic conveying or gravity discharge lines.

板式（筒式）、重力式、球型换向阀的切换角度和口径范围是多少？ What switching angles and size ranges do plate, gravity and ball type diverter valves have?

板式（筒式）：切换角度 45° 或 90°，DN50～DN500，筒式板阀流通阻力小、维护方便，适合常规粉料与颗粒料分路；重力式：切换角度 60°，DN50～DN500，依靠重力落料，适合大颗粒与大流量卸料工况；球型：切换角度 45°，DN50～DN300，球面密封、泄漏量低，适合对泄漏要求严格的场合。 Plate (cylinder) type: switching angle 45° or 90°, DN50–DN500 — low flow resistance and easy maintenance, for routine powder and pellet diverting. Gravity type: 60°, DN50–DN500 — gravity discharge for large particles and high-flow duty. Ball type: 45°, DN50–DN300 — spherical seal with low leakage, for applications with strict leakage limits.

换向阀的结构形式怎么选？ How should the diverter valve design be chosen?

优先看物料粒度与密封要求：含大颗粒或易架桥物料宜用重力式；对泄漏敏感的负压或高价值物料宜用球型；常规粉料与颗粒料分路以板式（筒式）更经济。同时需确认管路允许的安装角度，避免与设计倾角冲突。 Consider particle size and sealing requirement first: gravity type for materials with large particles or a tendency to bridge; ball type for vacuum or high-value products sensitive to leakage; plate (cylinder) type is the most economical for routine powder and pellet diverting. The permissible installation angle of the pipeline must also be confirmed so that it does not conflict with the design inclination.

换向阀选型需要确认哪些参数？ Which parameters must be fixed when selecting a diverter valve?

需同时确认口径、切换角度（45°/60°/90° 等）、工作压力与密封等级四类参数，并校核切换时是否存在物料滞留死角。含硬质颗粒的物料建议选用耐磨密封面并降低切换频率；真空或高背压工况需校核阀座两侧压差，避免切换卡涩。各结构均可按物料磨蚀性选配耐磨密封面与阀体材质。 Bore, switching angle (45°/60°/90° and others), working pressure and sealing class must be fixed together, and the valve checked for any dead zone where material could lodge during switching. For materials containing hard particles, wear-resistant sealing faces and a lower switching frequency are recommended. Under vacuum or high back-pressure duty the differential across the seat must be verified to avoid sticking. Every design can be supplied with wear-resistant sealing faces and body materials to suit abrasiveness.

换向阀支持阀位反馈、能接入控制系统吗？ Do diverter valves provide position feedback for connection to a control system?

可以。换向阀提供气动执行机构，双通道结构可选右侧信号箱（位置反馈），把阀位信号送入控制系统。 Yes. Diverter valves are supplied with pneumatic actuators, and the twin-channel design offers an optional right-hand signal box (position feedback) that feeds the valve position to the control system.

换向阀常用在哪些物料和行业？ Which materials and industries commonly use diverter valves?

常用于料仓多点进料、多点卸料以及输送管线切换。在 PP、PE、PVC、PTA 等物料系统中，换向阀承担成品仓分配与不合格料切换；在水泥、矿粉等场合用于多仓分配与旁路切换。 They are commonly used for multi-point silo feeding, multi-point discharge and pipeline switching. In systems handling PP, PE, PVC and PTA the diverter valve distributes product to finished-goods silos and switches off-spec material; in cement and mineral powder plants it handles multi-silo distribution and bypass switching.

除粉器（DeDuster）常见问题 DeDuster FAQ

除粉器的除粉原理、型号处理能力、适用物料与选型依据。 DeDuster working principle, model capacities, materials handled and selection criteria.

除粉器（DeDuster）是做什么用的？ What is a DeDuster used for?

除粉器用于去除颗粒物料在输送过程中产生的粉尘、静电粉尘与“拉丝”料，常安装在气力输送系统末端、料仓前或包装前工序。在塑料颗粒、树脂粒料等物料的气力输送与包装环节中，它是保证成品洁净度与后续加工稳定性的关键设备。 The DeDuster removes dust, electrostatic dust and streamers generated from pelletised material during conveying, and is commonly installed at the end of a pneumatic conveying system, ahead of the silo or before packing. In pneumatic conveying and packing of plastic pellets and resin granules it is the key equipment for ensuring finished-product cleanliness and stable downstream processing.

除粉器的工作原理是什么？ How does the DeDuster work?

带电颗粒进入磁场区后受“洛伦兹力”作用，颗粒与细粉之间的静电吸附被削弱；再配合气流作用，把吸附在颗粒表面的微细粉尘与拉丝剥离并带走。设备采用正压吹扫提供洁净气流，独特的窄缝与孔板设计实现高效拉丝去除，且不受物料流量波动影响。 Charged particles entering the magnetic flux field are acted on by the Lorentz force, which weakens the electrostatic bond between pellet and fine dust; the air flow then strips and carries away the micro-fine dust and streamers adhering to the pellet surface. The unit uses positive-pressure purging to provide a clean air stream, and a distinctive slot and hole design achieves efficient streamer removal that is not affected by fluctuations in material flow.

除粉器有哪些型号？处理能力多大？ Which DeDuster models are available, and what is their capacity?

标准型号 XD-20 / XD-30 / XD-40 / XD-50 / XD-60 / XD-90 的处理能力分别为 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 / 90 t/h，外形尺寸从 400W×700L×900H（XD-20）到 1100W×1150L×1350H（XD-90）；标准处理能力覆盖 10 kg/h 至 150 t/h，可按输送量选型。 Standard models XD-20 / XD-30 / XD-40 / XD-50 / XD-60 / XD-90 are rated at 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 / 90 t/h respectively, with dimensions from 400W×700L×900H (XD-20) to 1100W×1150L×1350H (XD-90); standard processing capacity spans 10 kg/h to 150 t/h and is selected to the conveying rate.

除粉器适用哪些物料？通常装在哪里？ Which materials does the DeDuster handle, and where is it installed?

广泛用于 PP、PE、PET、PVC 等颗粒物料的气力输送系统，与旋转阀、换向阀、料仓、包装机配套使用。在聚烯烃装置中，除粉器通常布置在成品料仓前或包装线上，用于控制产品粉尘含量与外观品质。 It is widely used in pneumatic conveying systems for pelletised materials such as PP, PE, PET and PVC, working alongside rotary valves, diverter valves, silos and packing machines. In polyolefin plants the DeDuster is normally positioned ahead of the product silo or on the packing line to control dust content and appearance quality.

除粉器选型依据是什么？ What are the selection criteria for a DeDuster?

主要依据输送量（t/h）、颗粒粒径与粉尘含量。处理能力需按峰值流量留有余量，同时确认现场供气压力与洁净度要求；对高静电物料需校核接地与吹扫气量。 Selection is based mainly on conveying rate (t/h), particle size and dust content. Processing capacity should have margin above peak flow, and the site air supply pressure and cleanliness requirement must be confirmed. For highly electrostatic materials, earthing and purge air rate need to be verified.

除粉器有哪些结构特点？ What are the design features of the DeDuster?

磁场与气流双重作用，微细粉尘与拉丝去除效率高；正压吹扫供气，运行稳定，不受流量波动影响；窄缝与孔板独特设计，适配不同粒径颗粒物料；标准处理能力覆盖 10 kg/h 至 150 t/h。 Combined magnetic field and air flow action gives high removal efficiency for micro-fine dust and streamers; positive-pressure purge air supply runs stably and is unaffected by flow fluctuation; a distinctive slot and hole design suits pellets of different particle sizes; and standard processing capacity spans 10 kg/h to 150 t/h.

失重秤配料与自动包装常见问题 Loss-in-Weight Feeding & Packaging FAQ

失重秤形式、配料精度、联锁与自动包装/拆包单元的选型要点。 Feeder types, batching accuracy, interlocks and selection points for automatic packing and bag-dumping units.

失重秤配料与自动包装系统包含什么？ What does a loss-in-weight feeding and automatic packaging system comprise?

它是气力输送系统前后两端的配套板块：前端按配方把多种物料按重量比例连续或批次投加，后端把成品定量装入吨包或小袋，全流程密闭以减少扬尘与物料损失。 It flanks the pneumatic conveying system at both ends: upstream, multiple components are dosed gravimetrically to recipe, continuously or in batches; downstream, finished product is filled into FIBCs or small bags by weight. The whole route is enclosed to cut dust and product loss.

失重秤有哪些形式？ Which types of loss-in-weight feeder are available?

包括失重秤、双螺杆失重称、皮带式失重称与液体失重秤，可按物料流动性与精度要求选择。 The range includes loss-in-weight feeders, twin-screw loss-in-weight feeders, belt-type loss-in-weight feeders and liquid loss-in-weight feeders, selected to material flowability and accuracy requirement.

失重秤配料系统的精度是多少？ What accuracy does the loss-in-weight batching system achieve?

批配料系统的出厂精度为 ±0.25%。高价值或高精度配方建议采用失重式连续计量，并保留校准与数据追溯功能。 Batch batching systems are factory-rated at ±0.25% accuracy. For high-value or high-accuracy recipes, continuous loss-in-weight dosing is recommended, retaining calibration and data traceability.

失重秤配料系统怎么与上下游联锁？ How does the feeding system interlock with upstream and downstream equipment?

配料系统与上游料仓、下游气力输送系统联锁，实现连续稳定供料。 The feeding system interlocks with upstream silos and the downstream pneumatic conveying system to deliver continuous, stable feed.

自动包装与拆包单元能做什么？ What do the automatic packing and bag-dumping units do?

自动吨包（FIBC）包装实现定量、脱气、封口一体化；自动拆包投料实现袋装原料自动破袋、投料与废袋排出。 Automatic FIBC (bulk bag) packing performs dosing, deaeration and sealing in one unit; automatic bag dumping opens, feeds and discharges waste bags from bagged raw material.

自动包装系统可以对接 AGV 与智能仓储吗？ Can the packing system interface with AGVs and automated warehousing?

可以。自动包装与拆包单元可与 AGV 智能转运、智能仓储衔接，构成少人化车间。 Yes. The automatic packing and bag-dumping units interface with AGV transfer and automated warehousing to form a low-manning plant.

配料系统与包装系统选型要先确定什么？ What must be fixed first when selecting the feeding and packing systems?

配料系统选型先确定配方组分数、单组分流量范围与精度要求，再确认物料流动性与架桥倾向；包装系统选型需明确包装规格、产能与粉尘控制要求。 For the feeding system, start by fixing the number of recipe components, the flow range of each and the accuracy required, then confirm flowability and bridging tendency. For packing, define bag format, capacity and dust control requirements.
