经营理念

源站发布：2014-12-03 · 静态快照：2026-10-24

上海科全工程技术有限公司的经营理念：以专业与诚信为本，用工程计算和现场经验解决粉体输送与配料问题；坚持快速响应、持续改进，为客户提供从工艺计算、设备选型到安装调试与备件供应的全流程服务。官网 www.shconque.com

上海科全工程技术有限公司（SCE）成立于 2010 年，注册资本 2000 万元，总部位于上海市松江区。公司以「专业、诚信、高效、持续改进」为经营理念，围绕粉粒体气力输送与散料处理设备，为石化、化工、矿物、食品等行业客户提供工程与设备服务。

Shanghai Conque Engineering Technology Co., Ltd. (SCE), founded in 2010 with registered capital of RMB 20 million and headquartered in Songjiang District, Shanghai, operates on the principles of professionalism, integrity, efficiency and continuous improvement, serving the petrochemical, chemical, mineral and food industries with pneumatic conveying and bulk material handling engineering.

专业立身 / Professionalism

公司深耕粉粒体气力输送领域，技术团队熟悉主流进口品牌设备的性能参数与选型要点。从单台阀门选型到整条输送系统设计，覆盖稀相、密相与中间相输送工况，口径范围 DN50–DN800、压力等级 1.0–6.0 bar。承接产品线包括旋转阀、高压旋转阀、双通道换向阀、除粉器与除粉系统、自动吨包机、气力输送系统与除尘设备。

Our technical team works on powder and pellet conveying, with detailed knowledge of the parameters and selection criteria of mainstream imported equipment. Scope ranges from a single valve selection to a complete conveying line, covering dilute, dense and strand phase conveying, size DN50–DN800 and pressure 1.0–6.0 bar. Product lines covered include rotary valves, high-pressure rotary valves, two-way diverter valves, pellet de-dusting units and dust removal systems, automatic FIBC bagging machines, pneumatic conveying systems and dust collection equipment.

Coperion 科倍隆（ZVD、ZVH、ZAQ、ZGM、ZKD、ZXD、ZXQ、ZRD 系列旋转阀；WHT、WRK、WET、WEK、WGY、WHK、WYK 系列换向阀）、Zeppelin 泽普林（旋转阀、换向阀、料仓与掺混系统、取样器）、Pelletron 派勒创除粉器（DeDuster，XD-20 至 XD-90）等品牌设备的性能参数与选型要点。 Technical parameters and selection criteria for Coperion rotary valves (ZVD/ZVH/ZAQ/ZGM/ZKD/ZXD/ZXQ/ZRD) and diverter valves (WHT/WRK/WET/WEK/WGY/WHK/WYK), Zeppelin rotary valves, diverter valves, silo and blending systems and samplers, and Pelletron DeDusters (XD-20 to XD-90).

诚信为本 / Integrity

报价、交货与售后各环节保持信息透明：设备来源、品牌型号、技术参数与交期以书面形式确认；进口设备与原装备件提供来源说明与技术支持，不做无法兑现的承诺，以长期合作而非单次交易为目标。

Quotations, delivery and after-sales are kept transparent: equipment source, brand model, technical parameters and lead time are confirmed in writing. Imported equipment and OEM spare parts come with source documentation and technical support. We commit only to what we can deliver, targeting long-term cooperation rather than one-off deals.

高效服务 / Efficiency

常用型号保持常备库存，缩短交付周期；售前提供选型计算与方案比对，售中跟进生产与发运节点，售后提供安装指导、调试运行与故障排查响应。

Fast-moving models are stocked to shorten lead times. We provide selection calculations and option comparison before order, follow production and shipping milestones during order, and respond with installation guidance, commissioning and troubleshooting after delivery.

持续创新 / Continuous Improvement

公司持续投入工程计算工具的开发与迭代，自主完成 PneuCalc 气力输送计算工具与散料装车计算平台；自主研发的 GVH 系列高压旋转阀实现对同类进口产品的替代，并通过 Rosneft、Daelim Engineering 等项目验收。

We keep investing in engineering software, having developed the PneuCalc pneumatic conveying tool and a bulk loading calculation platform in-house. Our GVH series high-pressure rotary valves substitute comparable imported products and have passed acceptance on Rosneft and Daelim Engineering projects.
