上海科全工程技术有限公司（SCE）成立于 2010 年，注册资本 2000 万元，总部位于上海市松江区，专注气力输送系统与散料处理设备的工程供应与技术服务。以下六项能力构成公司的核心优势。

Shanghai Conque Engineering Technology Co., Ltd. (SCE), founded in 2010 with registered capital of RMB 20 million and headquartered in Songjiang District, Shanghai, focuses on the engineering supply and technical service of pneumatic conveying systems and bulk material handling equipment. Six capabilities below define our core advantages.

产品范围 / Product Range

旋转阀、高压旋转阀、双通道换向阀、除粉器与除粉系统、自动吨包机、失重秤、气力输送系统、散料装车与除尘设备。

Rotary valves, high-pressure rotary valves, two-way diverter valves, pellet de-dusting units and dust removal systems, automatic FIBC bagging machines, loss-in-weight feeders, pneumatic conveying systems, bulk loading and dust collection equipment.

1. 进口品牌设备与原装备件供应 / Imported Brand Equipment & OEM Spare Parts

长期供应进口品牌旋转阀、高压旋转阀、双通道换向阀、除粉器与除粉系统等设备及原装备件，覆盖常用型号与压力等级，并提供选型比对、进口替代与技术支持。

We supply imported rotary valves, diverter valves and pellet de-dusting units together with OEM spare parts across the commonly used models and pressure ratings, and provide selection comparison, import substitution and technical support.

Coperion 科倍隆（ZVD、ZVH、ZAQ、ZGM、ZKD、ZXD、ZXQ、ZRD 系列旋转阀；WHT、WRK、WET、WEK、WGY、WHK、WYK 系列换向阀）、Zeppelin 泽普林（旋转阀、换向阀、料仓与掺混系统、取样器）、Pelletron 派勒创（除粉器 DeDuster，XD-20 至 XD-90）等进口设备与原装备件。 Shanghai Conque supplies imported equipment and OEM spare parts including Coperion rotary valves (ZVD/ZVH/ZAQ/ZGM/ZKD/ZXD/ZXQ/ZRD) and diverter valves (WHT/WRK/WET/WEK/WGY/WHK/WYK), Zeppelin rotary valves, diverter valves, silo and blending systems and samplers, and Pelletron DeDusters (XD-20 to XD-90).

2. 自研高压旋转阀替代进口 / In-house High-pressure Rotary Valves

自主研发 GVH 系列高压旋转阀，已在韩国大林工程公司（Daelim Engineering）与俄罗斯石油公司（Rosneft）的项目中通过验收，用于替代同类进口产品。

Our in-house GVH series high-pressure rotary valves have passed acceptance on projects of Daelim Engineering (Korea) and Rosneft (Russia), serving as substitutes for comparable imported products.

3. 工程计算与选型能力 / Engineering Calculation & Selection

自主开发 PneuCalc 气力输送计算工具与散料装车计算平台，覆盖稀相、密相与中间相输送的压降核算与风速限值校验，可输出设备选型与工艺参数。口径范围 DN50–DN800，压力等级 1.0–6.0 bar。

We develop the PneuCalc pneumatic conveying tool and a bulk loading calculation platform in-house, covering pressure-drop calculation and air-velocity limit checking for dilute, dense and strand phase conveying, and delivering equipment selection and process parameters. Size range DN50–DN800, pressure rating 1.0–6.0 bar.

4. 大型装置业绩 / Track Record on Large Plants

技术团队核心成员参与 40 万吨级 PVC、LDPE、HDPE 等聚烯烃装置的气力输送系统设计；服务客户包括 Rosneft、Daelim Engineering、上海石化、中国神华、陕西煤业等，产品出口俄罗斯、东南亚、中东等地区。

Core members of our technical team have taken part in the design of pneumatic conveying systems for 400,000 t/a PVC, LDPE and HDPE polyolefin plants. Customers include Rosneft, Daelim Engineering, Sinopec Shanghai, China Shenhua and Shaanxi Coal; products are exported to Russia, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

5. 备件库存与交付响应 / Spare Parts Stock & Delivery

常用型号保持常备库存，缩短交付周期；配备售后团队，提供安装指导、调试运行与故障排查的现场响应。

Fast-moving models are kept in stock to shorten lead times, backed by an after-sales team providing on-site installation guidance, commissioning and troubleshooting.

6. 全流程技术服务 / Full-cycle Engineering Service

从工艺计算、设备选型、方案设计到安装调试与备件供应，由同一技术团队贯穿负责，减少环节交接带来的信息损耗。

From process calculation, equipment selection and system design to installation, commissioning and spare parts supply, one technical team stays accountable throughout, reducing information loss at handovers.
